# 9 Curated NotebookLM Prompts for BMad Method

Use these high-impact prompts in the NotebookLM chat panel to explore, extract, and apply the BMad Method across your development workflow.

---

### 1. Five Essential Questions (Foundational Study)
> "Identify the 5 most critical foundational questions a software engineering lead must understand about the BMad Method before adopting it. For each question, provide an authoritative, cited answer detailing: (1) The 4-phase delivery loop (Clarify, Plan, Build & Verify, Learn & Adjust), (2) The concept of 'Well-Defined Intent', (3) The initiative directory structure (`_bmad-output`), (4) How `tickets.toml` drives deterministic implementation, and (5) The division of responsibilities among the specialized agent personas."

---

### 2. Find the Interesting Bits (Insight & Nuance Mining)
> "Analyze the entire BMad Method knowledge base and surface the top 5 most surprising, counter-intuitive, or unique architectural mechanisms that distinguish BMad from standard prompt engineering or generic AI coding assistants. Specifically analyze 'Party Mode' multi-agent debates, unattended build loops (`bmad-build-auto`), and the course correction protocol (`bmad-correct-course`)."

---

### 3. Interactive Quiz Show (Knowledge Mastery)
> "You are an expert examiner on the BMad Method. Challenge me with 5 scenario-based questions where I must select the exact BMad skill command, agent, or document template to use. Present one scenario at a time, wait for my response, grade it with strict fidelity to the documentation, and cite the exact file path from the repository."

---

### 4. Content Analyst Report (Executive & Team Briefing)
> "Synthesize a comprehensive Content Analyst Report evaluating the BMad Method for engineering leadership. Outline the core value proposition, developer experience (DX) impact, error reduction metrics, integration requirements for tools like Claude Code and Cursor, and governance over AI hallucination via structured specifications."

---

### 5. Community & Team Action Plan (Adoption Roadmap)
> "Produce a step-by-step rollout plan for introducing BMad to an engineering team working on both greenfield features and a legacy brownfield codebase. Detail: Week 1 setup and project context generation (`bmad-project-context`), Week 2 specification and story mapping (`bmad-spec` & `bmad-ticket`), Week 3 implementation and QA loops (`bmad-build` & `bmad-qa-generate-e2e-tests`), and Week 4 retrospective."

---

### 6. Technical Deep-Dive & Architecture Specification
> "Provide a technical deep-dive into how BMad manages state and configuration across sessions. Compare `customize.toml` vs `config.template.toml`, explain how user overrides in `_bmad/custom/` take precedence, and map out the file hierarchy of an initiative folder."

---

### 7. 360° Critique & Trade-off Analysis
> "Perform a 360-degree evaluation of BMad against traditional Scrum/Kanban and raw AI agent coding. Highlight key strengths (rigorous boundaries, ticket trees, multi-persona review) and identify potential failure modes or anti-patterns (such as over-specifying trivial changes or skipping the review gate)."

---

### 8. Developer & PM Onboarding Curriculum (Study Kit)
> "Create an onboarding curriculum for a new developer and a new product manager joining a BMad-powered repository. Provide daily learning modules, key skills to run, expected document artifacts to review (`spine-template.md`, `prd-template.md`, `spec-template.md`), and exercises."

---

### 9. Keynote / Conference Talk Script
> "Draft an inspiring, technical 10-minute conference talk script titled 'The BMad Method: How Specialized AI Agents and Agile Contracts Build Real Software'. Include speaker cues, slide descriptions, concrete code/ticket examples, and an explanation of the Clarify-Plan-Build-Learn cycle."
